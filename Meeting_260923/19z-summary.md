# <span class="cat intro">Intro</span> What to carry out of chapter 1

- **An experiment is three steps.** We prepare a known state, apply a unitary, and measure.
- **The unitary is the part we design on purpose.**
- **The response is not the sequence alone.** It also carries the configuration of the system.

<div class="callout">

The measured curve is the designed response combined with the structure of the system, and
that combination is a convolution.

</div>
