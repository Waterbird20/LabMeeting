# References

<div class="ref">

1. J. M. Martyn, Z. M. Rossi, A. K. Tan, I. L. Chuang, *Grand Unification of Quantum Algorithms*. PRX Quantum **2**, 040203 (2021). `arXiv:2105.02859`. QSP theorem, Chebyshev pair, reachability, Appendix-D phase lists.
2. D. Motlagh, N. Wiebe, *Generalized Quantum Signal Processing*. PRX Quantum **5**, 020368 (2024). `arXiv:2308.01501`. Laurent form of the theorem and the peel-off recursion.
3. Our conditional-gate study: `3. wiki/projects/qsp/conditional-gate.md`, `3. wiki/concepts/qsp-phase-filter-design.md` (2026-09). Detuning as the signal, block structure, degree equals number of waits.
4. T. H. Taminiau, J. J. T. Wagenaar, T. van der Sar, F. Jelezko, V. V. Dobrovitski, R. Hanson, *Detection and control of individual nuclear spins using a weakly coupled electron spin*. Phys. Rev. Lett. **109**, 137602 (2012). Conditional Hamiltonian, CPMG branch propagators, $P_x=(1+M)/2$, resonance condition, spin 3 of Table I.
5. L. M. Pham, *Magnetic Field Sensing with Nitrogen-Vacancy Color Centers in Diamond*. Ph.D. thesis, Harvard University (2013), Ch. 1. NV level structure, ESR/ODMR, Rabi, Ramsey, $T_2^*$, $T_2$, $T_1$; Figs. 1.4, 1.5, 1.7 reused.
6. M. A. Nielsen, I. L. Chuang, *Quantum Computation and Quantum Information*, Cambridge (2010), Chs. 1, 2, 4. Textbook material of section 1.
7. Convolution and DDrf side peaks, lab meeting 2026-09-03 (`3. wiki/labmeeting/260903/`): the product-to-convolution derivation and the dice, polynomial and Fourier clips (rendered from 3Blue1Brown's *But what is a convolution?* source, MIT).
8. J. Haah, *Product decomposition of periodic functions in quantum signal processing*. Quantum **3**, 190 (2019); QSPPACK (Y. Dong *et al.*, Phys. Rev. A **103**, 042419 (2021)); pyqsp (`github.com/ichuang/pyqsp`).
9. New clips (`bloch-plus`, `seq-rabi`, `seq-ramsey`, `seq-odmr`, `bell-mixed`, `dd-cpmg`, `qsp-coeff-conv`) and the stepping figure: `3. wiki/code/lm-260923-animations/`, manim community 0.21, every state and axis computed from the actual matrices.
