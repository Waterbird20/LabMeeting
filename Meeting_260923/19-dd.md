# <span class="cat intro">Intro</span> Dynamical decoupling: make the conditional operation unconditional

$$
H=\ket{0}\bra{0}\otimes H_0+\ket{1}\bra{1}\otimes H_1
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
U(t)=e^{-iHt}=\ket{0}\bra{0}\otimes U_0(t)+\ket{1}\bra{1}\otimes U_1(t) .
$$

<div class="columns">
<div class="col">

For one ${}^{13}$C near an NV centre, $H_0=\omega_L I_z$ and $H_1=(\omega_L+A_\parallel)\,I_z+A_\perp I_x$. A $\pi$ pulse swaps the two blocks, so the CPMG unit $\tau-\pi-2\tau-\pi-\tau$ interleaves them:

$$
V_0=U_0\,U_1U_1\,U_0,\qquad V_1=U_1\,U_0U_0\,U_1 .
$$

Both are unitary, so the only question is whether the two rotations agree. At a generic $\tau$ they do: $V_0=V_1$, the electron sees no difference between its branches, and $M=1$. Only special spacings break that, as the next slide shows.
