# Outline

1. **Double quantum with an unpolarized $^{14}$N.** 
- The unpolarized  nucleus gives three detunings, and one $\pi$ pulse cannot serve all three.
2. **Quantum signal processing.** 
- Pulses and equal waits turn the detuning into a polynomial in terms of detuning
3. **Applications.** 
   - Universal NOT on the single-quantum qubit $\ket{0}\leftrightarrow\ket{{-1}}$.
   - DQ state preparation.
   - DQ $\pi$ pulse.
