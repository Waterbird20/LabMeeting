# References used by the ml section (10-section-ml.md, 11-what-is-ml.md, 12-uat.md)

1. G. Cybenko, "Approximation by superpositions of a sigmoidal function," *Mathematics of Control, Signals and Systems* **2**, 303–314 (1989). DOI: 10.1007/BF02551274. Uniform approximation of continuous functions on the unit cube by one-hidden-layer networks with a continuous sigmoidal activation. Cited on 12-uat.md.
2. K. Hornik, "Approximation capabilities of multilayer feedforward networks," *Neural Networks* **4**(2), 251–257 (1991). DOI: 10.1016/0893-6080(91)90009-T. Theorem 2: any continuous, bounded and nonconstant activation gives density in $C(X)$ for every compact $X\subset\mathbb{R}^k$ (checked against the PDF, p. 252). Cited on 12-uat.md.
3. M. Leshno, V. Ya. Lin, A. Pinkus and S. Schocken, "Multilayer feedforward networks with a nonpolynomial activation function can approximate any function," *Neural Networks* **6**(6), 861–867 (1993). DOI: 10.1016/S0893-6080(05)80131-5. Universality holds if and only if the activation is not a polynomial (covers ReLU). Not on a slide; only in the EDIT-FORWARD note of 12-uat.md.
